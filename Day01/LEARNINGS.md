# AI Research Learnings

## 1. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computer
systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
These tasks can include learning from information, recognizing
patterns, understanding language, making decisions, and solving
problems.

AI is used in many areas of everyday life and different industries,
including healthcare, finance, education, marketing, transportation,
and entertainment.

## 8. Types of AI

### Narrow AI

Narrow AI is designed to perform specific tasks. Most AI systems
currently used in real-world applications are Narrow AI.

Examples include recommendation systems, voice assistants, fraud
detection systems, and AI-based medical image analysis.

### General AI

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is a future conceptual form of
AI that would be capable of performing a broad range of intellectual
tasks similar to human intelligence.

AGI does not currently exist as a demonstrated general-purpose
technology.

## 9. AI Domains and Applications

### Healthcare

AI can assist healthcare professionals by analyzing medical data and
medical images to support diagnosis.

### Finance

AI can analyze financial transactions and identify unusual patterns
that may indicate fraud.

### Education

AI can provide personalized learning materials based on a student's

### Marketing

## 10. Fields of AI

### Machine Learning

Machine Learning allows computer systems to learn patterns from data
and use those patterns to make predictions or decisions.

### Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a specialized area of Machine Learning that uses
multi-layered neural networks to learn complex patterns.

### AI Engineering

AI Engineering focuses on building, deploying, monitoring, maintaining,
and improving AI systems for real-world applications.

---

## 11. AI Technologies

### Natural Language Processing

NLP allows computers to understand, process, and generate human
language.

Examples include chatbots, translation, sentiment analysis, and text
summarization.

### Speech Recognition

Speech Recognition converts spoken language into text or commands.

Examples include voice assistants, voice typing, and automatic
transcription.

### Computer Vision

Computer Vision enables computers to interpret information from images
and videos.

Examples include face recognition, object detection, and medical
image analysis.

## 12. Role of an AI Engineer in 2026

An AI Engineer builds and maintains AI-powered systems for real-world
use.

Key responsibilities include:

* Preparing and processing data.
* Developing and training AI models.
* Evaluating model performance.
* Integrating AI models into applications.
* Deploying AI systems.
* Monitoring system performance.
* Maintaining and improving deployed systems.
* Considering reliability, privacy, security, and responsible AI use.

The AI Engineer works throughout the AI lifecycle:

**Data → Development → Training → Evaluation → Deployment →
Monitoring → Maintenance**

---

## 13. Training vs. Inference

### Training

Training is the process through which an AI model learns patterns
from training data.

**Example:** A fraud detection model learns patterns from historical
transactions labeled as legitimate or fraudulent.

### Inference

Inference is the process of using a trained model to make predictions
on new data.

**Example:** A trained fraud detection model analyzes a new
transaction and predicts whether it may be fraudulent.

In simple terms:

**Training = Learning**

**Inference = Prediction**
