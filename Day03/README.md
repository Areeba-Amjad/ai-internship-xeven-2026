# Task 3: What is AI and the Role of an AI Engineer

## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. AI can help computers understand information, recognize patterns, learn from examples, and make useful decisions. We interact with AI in many ways every day. For example, YouTube and Netflix recommend videos and movies based on what we watch. Voice assistants can understand spoken questions, while online stores recommend products we may like.

AI is important because it can help people solve problems faster, automate repetitive tasks, and make better use of large amounts of information. It is being used in areas such as healthcare, finance, education, transportation, and marketing. In healthcare, for example, AI can help doctors examine medical images and identify possible health problems.

An AI Engineer is a person who builds and maintains AI-powered systems. Their work includes preparing information, creating and testing AI solutions, connecting them with applications, and making sure they work correctly. AI Engineers also monitor systems after they are launched and improve them when needed.

In simple terms, AI helps computers perform intelligent tasks, while AI Engineers are responsible for turning these capabilities into useful and reliable real-world solutions.
